## Executive summary

The project began with a broad question:

> Can a coding actor remain grounded and effective when the task world and
> exact interaction chronology are larger than its active context window?

The current answer is a qualified **yes**.

The project has demonstrated that Qwen3.8-27B-AD-IQ2_S can continue grounded
coding work after authentic context pressure when the host preserves complete
exact custody outside the prompt and reconstructs a bounded decision frame
containing:

1. exact authorized purpose and active step;
2. exact current candidate and resource state;
3. readable path/symbol orientation;
4. one ordered, signal-bearing event plane;
5. current candidate/environment bindings;
6. canonical addresses for exact demand-loaded payloads;
7. a compact capability contract; and
8. a hard-capped 512-token private reasoning allowance that is not carried
   into later chronology.

Experiment 020 is the strongest ecological result. Across two fresh
owner-controlled repairs, two seeds, and paired R50/X25 branches, all eight
branches produced hidden-correct candidates, passed public checks, and
submitted. X25 reduced peak resident prompt size by 25–41% while preserving
quality.

The economic result is equally important: X25 used more calls and did not
reduce aggregate prompt processing. Exact externalization is therefore earned
as a **capacity and executability mechanism**, not as an unconditional token-
cost optimization.

The project has converged on a simple policy:

> Keep exact payload bodies resident while they fit. At authentic pressure,
> externalize the oldest bulky bodies while retaining readable event signal,
> exact validity bindings, and one canonical access address. Let the model
> decide what exact evidence to reacquire.

No semantic summary, relationship graph, embedding/ranker, host-selected
relevance, model-authored memory, or automatic duplicate suppression has
earned inclusion.

## What has been established

### Strongly established

- Complete chronology can remain authoritative outside the active context.
- Exact purpose and current-world state can be projected without a semantic
  summary.
- Readable native code identities provide useful orientation.
- Dynamic observations are first-class information and require exact
  candidate/environment validity bindings.
- Qwen can choose a current observation over a stale exact observation, reopen
  the body, use the recovered fact in a mutation, check, and submit.
- One exact monotonic event plane is more reliable than separate progress
  clocks.
- Large historical action and result payloads can be externalized while their
  model-usable signal remains resident.
- Canonical payload identity prevents provenance/address indirection without
  suppressing explicit model-selected reacquisition.
- A 25k decision frame can preserve grounded quality while materially reducing
  peak resident occupancy.

### Supported with important limits

- The controller works across recurrent authentic boundaries, but the number
  of independent task geometries remains small.
- P0 scales through hierarchical pages in controlled larger worlds, but has
  not been validated against an organically large and unfamiliar repository.
- Exact event signal can preserve progress, but its presence does not guarantee
  that Qwen will integrate every completed event or avoid conservative rereads.
- Low bounded reasoning is part of the successful tested bundle; its value is
  not a claim that every task benefits from exactly 512 tokens.
- X25 improves peak capacity and headroom. It may or may not reduce total
  prompt processing, calls, or endpoint time.

### Not established

- reliable preservation of a complex evolving hypothesis, rejected
  alternatives, or a long causal plan across boundaries;
- dense choice among many simultaneously current and plausible observations;
- user revision of a parent objective after several completed stages;
- repository-scale P0 discovery when relevant targets are not strongly named;
- generality beyond this Qwen actor, quantization, sampler, reasoning envelope,
  Python domain, and tool contract;
- superiority to an unlimited-context actor;
- a general semantic memory or lossy-compaction mechanism.

## What Qwen's transcripts taught us

The durable lesson is not merely that smaller prompts can work.

### Signal and payload have different jobs

Qwen needs resident signal sufficient to decide where exact grounding might
live. Useful resident fields were ordinary, readable concepts:

- action or observation type;
- readable target;
- accepted/passed/completed status;
- candidate, file, check, or environment binding;
- exact range and completion state;
- payload class, size/hash, and canonical address.

The address alone is not information. Conversely, a relationship or event row
being truthful does not make it useful if the model cannot connect it to the
current decision.

### Exact reacquisition is behaviorally conservative

When exact bodies left residence, Qwen often reopened or reread them before
acting even though the event plane recorded that acquisition had occurred.
This was sometimes economically expensive, but it preserved quality in all
four Experiment 020 X25 paths. The project therefore does not automatically
suppress, cache-substitute, or reinterpret repeated requests.

### Capacity and cumulative efficiency are distinct

Experiment 018 showed that externalization can make continuation possible when
resident history cannot fit. Experiment 020 showed that, when both conditions
fit, resident bodies can avoid later acquisition calls. A system should choose
externalization based on active-envelope pressure, not assume it is always the
cheapest representation.

### Model success depends on executable headroom

Several earlier failures occurred after Qwen had the correct purpose, evidence,
and intended patch but lacked room for correction, recheck, and submission.
Future task qualification must reserve an actual correction-and-closure cycle,
not merely prove that one ideal path fits.

## Host and methodology lessons

The project repeatedly found that apparatus labels alone can misdescribe model
behavior. The governing process is now:

```text
seal exact responses
-> replay and mechanical grading
-> inspect every prompt/reasoning/action/result
-> audit each host transition
-> separate apparatus validity from actor behavior
-> synthesize paired outcomes and mechanisms
-> only then make a project decision
```

Important host defects found and corrected prospectively included:

- lexical canonical-JSON enforcement not required by the model contract;
- missing pretransport request custody on terminal failures;
- runtime token-accounting mismatch handling;
- undisclosed legal public-check identifiers;
- public-check import-path defects;
- M0's hidden relationship-extractor dependency in the earlier metadata line;
- operator liveness monitoring scoped to the wrong branch;
- non-idempotent content-addressed snapshot reuse and accepted no-op patches;
- reopen operations manufacturing competing payload addresses.

Historical affected runs remain immutable and explicitly unscorable or
qualified. A later fix never retroactively converts them into clean results.

The project is no longer searching broadly for a metadata system. It has a
working minimal information architecture:

```text
history is exact custody
purpose and current world are resident
orientation is compact and readable
progress is one ordered exact signal plane
large evidence is demand-loaded
reasoning is bounded and ephemeral
actions and effects remain exactly bound
```

Experiment 020 supplies a clean ecological reliability result for that
architecture. The representation micro-ablation phase is closed unless a new
repeated failure earns reopening it.

The remaining research problem is behavioral and ecological:

> Can the same frozen controller support genuinely long, strategically complex
> work across more varied repositories, observations, and purpose changes
> without losing grounding or closure?

1. strategic continuity across an evolving hypothesis and rejected
   alternatives;
2. repository-scale hierarchical P0 discovery;

5. several authentic pressure boundaries in one ecological task.

Do not combine every gap in the first resumed study. Select a real task first,
then prospectively state which one or two gaps it tests.

### Keep the comparison causal

Where authentic pressure exists, retain the paired comparison:

```text
R50: same signal-bearing event plane, exact bodies resident while physically fit
X25: same ancestry and event plane, oldest bulky bodies externalized only at pressure
```

Both branches should receive the same controller and low bounded reasoning.
The comparison is about resident-body policy under a hard envelope, not about
giving one branch better semantic state.

### Entry gates for any successor

Before new model exposure:

- use a fresh unexposed task or explicitly classify the work as post-hoc;
- freeze exact acceptance semantics and independently test the checker;
- verify enough action and token headroom for one full correction cycle;
- bind actor, runtime, controller, bank/package, schedule, and executable
  closure;
- prove byte-identical paired ancestry before the pressure fork;
- preserve one attempt, no retry/repair/rescue, exact sealing, and evaluator
  separation;
- require the full receipt, host-path audit, apparatus finding, direct
  transcript audit, and results decision before another authorization.

### Let repeated failures earn the next mechanism

| Repeated ecological failure | Next question that would become earned |
|---|---|
| Event signal itself crowds the frame | Hierarchical or paged event root |
| Completed events are repeatedly ignored and cause outcome loss | Current-state or confirmation-policy diagnostic |
| P0 cannot locate relevant code at scale | Repository-scale literal search/expansion design |
| Exact progress survives but an evolving causal plan is lost | Bounded strategic-continuity artifact |
| Current and stale evidence are confused | Stronger mechanical validity presentation |
| Correct work reaches checks too late to close | Explicit resource/closure planning study |
| User changes invalidate earlier purpose | Purpose-revision and authority protocol |

Do not build these mechanisms in advance.
