was only 12,123 tokens and therefore did not create a boundary. A later tuning
overshot and denied `fork_ready` itself. The final geometry uses two 192-row
custody ledgers, retaining pressure while preserving the eight-action path.

Python's standard-library module and made its valid Phase-B patch uncheckable.

the preseal artifacts were reconstructed, and both public/hidden paths passed.
