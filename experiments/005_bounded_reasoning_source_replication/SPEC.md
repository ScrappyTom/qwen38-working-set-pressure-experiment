# Experiment 005: fresh bounded-reasoning source replication

## Question

Does server-bounded low private reasoning help the exact Qwen actor use readable
P0 symbol ranges to reacquire governing source economically after a constructed
25k reconstruction boundary?

## Conditions

- R0: reasoning off.
- R1: reasoning enabled, low effort, server-enforced maximum 512 private tokens
  per call.

Within each case both branches receive the same exact constructed fork:
candidate, fork binding, latest action/result, P0, observation directory,
verbatim Phase B pointer, tools, seed, sampler, and eight-call horizon. No
semantic summary or host-selected relevant fact is supplied.

## Bank and schedule

Two fresh source-reacquisition geometries are constructed before actor exposure.
Each has a large governing source with a familiar getter symbol and a top-level
constant, a small mutation target, a dependent consumer, public and hidden
checks, and an evaluator-only known requirement.

One seed is used per geometry. Branch order alternates. Four branches are run,
one attempt each, with zero retries, repairs, or rescues.

## Interpretation

This is a constructed post-reset diagnostic, not an authentic live pressure
event. Quality and closure are primary. Exact read bytes, cumulative and peak
prompt tokens, completion tokens, calls, latency, and direct reasoning/action
alignment are secondary.

Promotion requires a repeated directional source-economy advantage without a
quality regression. A single attractive trace is insufficient.
