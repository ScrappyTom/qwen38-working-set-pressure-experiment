binding, and owner authorization qualify offline with zero model, endpoint, or
GPU calls.

produced the exact known-good candidate, ran a current-candidate public check,
and submitted in both fresh geometries. The stale-check case prospectively

`T25-UNIFIED` also assigns it the next mechanical receipt sequence. Both retain
the exact body and reopen handle. No semantic host field or suppression is
introduced.

The authorization is the owner's instruction `Proceed`. It permits exactly
eight fresh measured branches, one attempt each, and no retry, repair, rescue,
same-bank rerun, or automatic successor.
