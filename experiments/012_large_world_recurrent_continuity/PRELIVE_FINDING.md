# Experiment 012 pre-live finding

## Disposition

The larger-world apparatus passes offline construction and scripted
qualification. No actor, endpoint, GPU, or model server was used.

The sealed bank contains two fresh 160-file candidates, four exact
user-authored phases per task, two frozen seeds, four shared prefixes, and
eight C50/T25 branches. Candidate payloads are roughly 2.2 MiB each. The
top-level P0 roots contain eighteen readable entries and remain below their
6,000-byte bound; deeper directory and file-outline pages are deterministic,
bounded, task-independent, and source-free.

## What changed and why

The prior 20-file candidate admission and complete 8,000-byte P0 inventory
could not express the authorized larger-world question. Experiment 012 raises
only the candidate geometry ceiling and introduces hierarchical P0 pages. It
does not add summaries, rankings, relationships, embeddings, caching,
duplicate suppression, or host semantic selection.

Qwen still chooses which directory, file, symbol, source start, or observation
to inspect. The host chooses only exact identity, validity binding, capacity,
and deterministic physical page size.

## Scripted qualification

Both scripted T25 paths completed Phases B, C, and D, passed every public and
hidden check, and submitted the exact known-good candidate. Each required four
reconstruction events across the carried-forward initial boundary and later
intra/inter-phase pressure events.

Both scripted C50 paths hit the frozen physical context during Phase B. That is
an expected capacity reference, not a quality claim. The same scripted actions
remained executable in T25 after exact reconstruction.

The qualification used the real tokenizer, complete request renderer, 25k and
50,176-token guards, hierarchical P0 executor, maximal exact reads, patch and
check isolation, observation custody, candidate bindings, and stage budgets.

## Live boundary

The measured run is authorized only after the exact executable closure and
owner record are committed. It permits one pass through the four-cell frozen
schedule, one attempt per branch, no retries, repairs, rescues, summaries, or
automatic successor. Every prompt, private reasoning field, action, result,
candidate, observation, and capacity denial must be read directly before a
behavioral conclusion is written.
