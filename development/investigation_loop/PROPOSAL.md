## What earns a later change

Incorrectly used or unavailable returns earn a focused host/interface repair.
Repeated operational confusion about what a byte count or hash measures can
earn clearer scope labels or omission from the normal decision view, while
retaining operation-required bindings and internal custody. Grouping ideas remain
deferred, not disproven by faulty model-generated examples. No further vocabulary
study is required to observe these issues during ordinary work.

Evidence lost through externalization may earn selection/recovery work; an
unretained working rationale may earn an explicit working account; failure to
use available evidence may concern reasoning or action choice. These remedies
must follow the actual distinction, not be prebuilt together. Keep task outcome,
first-check result, correction opportunity, reacquisition, repetition, input,
generation and time separate. No larger architecture follows from this pilot.
