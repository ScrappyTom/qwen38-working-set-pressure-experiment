# Lessons to carry forward from verifier run003

1. Qualify transformations through their consumer. A source spelling classified
   as relative by PurePosixPath can become drive-relative when its components are
   joined by a native Windows Path. Check the actual safety property after that
   composition, not only a growing list of familiar spellings. The expanded
   checker repaired one known omission but still missed this boundary.
2. Keep operational completion and substantive task acceptance separate. A
   delivered pass, correct submission and exact replay establish the operating
   sequence. A separately demonstrated contract violation keeps the task open.
   Preserve old verdicts; publish a new checker/job identity for new qualification.
3. Preserve obligation time. Before-first-edit inspection, source presently
   available for editing, and current verification are different facts. Do not
   force the actor to recompute their relationship on every call. This run exposes
   repeated prior-dispatch/current-input reconciliation without an incorrect read
   gate. Any display improvement must preserve actual dispatch semantics.
4. Measure a recovery transition by what follows it. Here a real delivered
   capacity rejection led to a model-selected group, exact target reacquisition,
   edits, actual failed-check correction and submission. That is stronger than
   a scripted fitting group. It does not demonstrate every pressure condition.
5. Saved interpretation is fallible. Preserve authorship and relationships but do
   not promote copied status, estimated coordinates or a claim of repair into
   source or execution evidence. Wrong account coordinates caused extra work;
   stale closure text did not override the actual current pass.
6. Inspect actual extents before making delivery claims. The reviewer briefly
   mistook end_line=0 for full delivery. Returned1-46/55 plus next_start47 proved
   otherwise. Preserve and correct evaluator mistakes just as actor mistakes.
7. Count complete costs and preserve limits. Twenty requests/29 operations include
   one rejected read, nine account updates and two checks. The new containment
   review is additional development evidence. Replaying the same check is not
   another task success; a successor must retain all prior cost and assistance.

These are recorded lessons, not a mandate for a new memory system, mandatory
accounts, different reasoning effort or automatic semantic repair. The immediate
earned work is checker composition and an explicitly reopened containment job.
