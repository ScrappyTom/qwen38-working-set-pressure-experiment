## Change

Add an opt-in presentation mixin for prospective phase adapters. Keep the frozen
historical classes and their replay behavior unchanged.

1. Project required-source coverage from the prior delivery ledger plus exact
   current-version source actually included in the constructed input. Label the
   basis explicitly. Rendering must be pure: neither admission trials nor inspection
   of a proposed view commits delivery. The normal mark_delivered boundary remains
   responsible for the ledger, and editing authority remains based on actual source.
   Do not credit retained but omitted selections, source-looking accounts, incomplete
   serialized historical pages, or old file versions. Complete recovered source can
   contribute only under the existing exact recovery rule.
2. Include the recorded resolved path in recent accepted region-edit rows. Obtain
   it from the preserved effect, never by resolving a historical region against the
   current candidate. Preserve candidate bindings, component outcomes, and history.
   A rejected request must not acquire an invented target or successful effect.

Do not add instructions to count lines, mandatory accounts, semantic status inference,
new selection policies, or a reasoning-setting change. The host supplies facts;
the model still decides whether the information is understood or the work adequate.
