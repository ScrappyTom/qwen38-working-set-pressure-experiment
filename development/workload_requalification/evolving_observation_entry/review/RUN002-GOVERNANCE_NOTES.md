# Lessons to retain from run002

* Artifact correctness, complete source delivery, and a specified recovery procedure
  are distinct outcomes. A current public pass covers the registered executable
  behavior; it does not certify all procedural obligations in the task text.
* Advancing pages on the same paths is useful acquisition, not repetition. Inspect
  actual returned ranges before classifying the action. Here five disjoint groups
  delivered four complete ledgers.
* An account can retain a useful value and cumulative coverage while omitting the
  condition that governs later use. Measure the distinction needed by the next
  decision, not account presence or freshness alone.
* A full replacement API makes an empty results list consequential. Before changing
  its policy, establish whether the actor understands that consequence. The present
  transcript omits discussion of release; it does not establish its cause.
* Keep the original task contract separate from an evaluator's stronger inference.
  Full ledger delivery is required by submission; the task does not require it
  before each unrelated edit. Correcting that evaluator does not excuse the separate
  missed pre-footer recovery condition.
* A cheaper, more complete acquisition path and a remaining task miss may coexist.
  Preserve both. Do not call this a full success, a model incapacity verdict, or
  proof that either acquisition patch alone caused the cost difference.
* Replay precedes dependent assessment. Both precede reporting a sealed result.
  Direct input/output review remains necessary even when the artifact passes.
