## Bounded implementation

sources or historical task adapters. Ordinary inputs remain byte-identical.
In recovery, offer the same last-six-operation window and existing exact navigation
projection, with the existing shared byte/page allowance. No semantic ranking,
model summary, inferred dependency, account rewrite or forced source selection.

Each record remains historical, with its original candidate and scope. Search hits
and addresses do not become full source or editing authority. Current-file
fingerprint comparisons retain their narrow meaning. Deduplicate detail already
present in immediate feedback. Preserve the exact archive and checker policy.

existing recovery view exactly, so neither large navigation pages nor many archived
actions can prevent delivery of essential feedback or a selection change. Use the

Do not also redesign ordinary search summaries, payload transport, reasoning effort,
account policy, or acquisition ordering. C20's ordinary zero-match omission remains
a separate observed tradeoff, not silently solved by a recovery-only change.
