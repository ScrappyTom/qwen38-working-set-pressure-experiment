# Operable recovery: saved tests, failed correction

The observation/recovery implementation is retained. The sole uncoached attempt
executes a model-selected recovery transition, saves an additive regression method,
receives an actual failed coverage check, and retrieves its preserved details.
It does **not** complete the tests-and-documentation contribution. C13 misinterprets
the fault-test details and exhausts physical context without a final operation.

The plan was published at `5fe48c51`; implementation, qualification and the exact
run package were published at `70e6e21ce7d2a24c593477b51dfda300610b4780` before
exposure. No implementation or setting changed during the attempt. Three requests
and thirty-two operations close unused; this package has no retry or continuation.

## What was implemented and qualified

Exact paged inspection reaches those observations without the old double-serialization
limit. Execution and presentation outcomes remain separate. Storage/capture limits
and incomplete observations are explicit; the old path remains a historical default.

The new recovery presentation retains source/result designations while temporarily
omitting their bulk bodies. It presents the actual obstacle, current bindings,
account and selection inventory, and permits model-chosen inspection/replacement.
Editing authority follows actual source delivery. Discovery exposes reusable exact
region references; complete region assembly rejects rather than silently shortening

[Preparation](../PREPARATION.md) records 94 selected passing checks, eleven native
grammar cases, exact observation inspection and scripted complete/correction routes.
Those routes establish preservation, transitions and supplied information paths;
they are not autonomous model outcomes. No proposal channel, payload-format change,
reasoning cap, automatic semantic summary or new planner was introduced.

## What the actual inputs explain

The [full transcript review](TRANSCRIPT_REVIEW.md) distinguishes temporary source
turnover, incomplete maintained understanding and persistent interpretation errors.
The host initially shortens even a small account to 512 bytes in recovery despite
ample input room. Qwen notices the cut text, reacquires source and copies a weakened
snippet into its next account. Separately, it fails to retain or derive the empty-port
consequence of the previously displayed normalization implementation. Reacquiring
that implementation eventually resolves the wrong assumption before editing.

The final failure differs. The saved test, relevant implementation and exact fault
record are visible. Qwen sometimes recognizes that failing under an injected fault
is desired, then reverses that interpretation and tries to make every mutation run
exercise every path. The actual checker requires complete paths only in the normal
run, plus detection of each mutation. Its elaborate private replacements still accept
ValueError subclasses. Those drafts were neither emitted nor executed.

The check report's count of six detected faults does not name the missed criterion.
Raw nested fields describe *test-run success*, which is the opposite of detection
success under an injected fault. These facts are correct, but their scopes require
interpretation that the response repeatedly gets wrong. This earns a more explicit
criterion/detection report, not a claim that inaccessible evidence caused this stop.
The whole response cost cannot be assigned to that presentation alone: it also
contains unsupported unittest theories, later-task planning and repeated serialization.

Three optional accounts are saved, but the last still lists acquisition as pending
after the source has arrived and after the test is saved. No account reflects the
failed check. Their custody and availability work; productive maintenance and benefit
through correction remain unestablished. Neither automatic checks nor accounts can
guarantee an executable, correct next decision.

## Decision

Retain the observation boundary, recovery arrangement, source guards and recorded
effects. The live route supports more than scripted feasibility, while the incomplete
task remains a failure of the combined operating configuration.

Two narrow prospective changes are earned: show the complete small account when it
fits before falling back to a bounded prefix, and report the failed check criterion
and named undetected faults explicitly, while preserving raw observations. Neither
change is silently applied to this frozen run. A future evaluation should test actual
failed-check interpretation through an emitted correction and its result before
another long whole-contribution attempt. An outside-run interpretation consultation,
if used to settle the presentation, must remain separate from uncoached execution.

Do not repeat this unchanged attempt with more requests, salvage private drafts, or
attribute a future improvement to one component of this multi-part configuration.
Action completion under medium uncapped thinking remains unresolved. Format or runtime
reasoning-boundary alternatives still require their own declared qualification.
