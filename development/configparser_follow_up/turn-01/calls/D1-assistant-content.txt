### 2. Operation limits and expressibility

Relevant supplied limits for `patch` are:

- `patch` must have exactly these keys:
  - `action`
  - `path`
  - `old`
  - `new`
  - `expected_candidate_id`
  - `expected_file_sha256`
- `path` must identify an existing file and satisfy the path limits.
- `old` and `new` may be 0 to 512 characters, and each is also limited to 2,000 UTF-8 bytes.
- `old` must occur exactly once.
- `old` and `new` must differ.
- `expected_candidate_id` and `expected_file_sha256` must match the current **pre-edit** candidate ID and file fingerprint.
- The effective diff must fit 6,000 UTF-8 bytes.
- The final action must fit 5,000 UTF-8 bytes.
- The successor candidate must satisfy the supplied candidate limits, including file-size and line-size limits.

For the supplied strings:

| String value | Characters / UTF-8 bytes |
|---|---:|
| Shared `old` | 194 |
| Proposed `new` | 540 |
| Actual final `new` | 512 |

Therefore:

- The shared `old` is within the 512-character limit.
- The actual final `new` is exactly within the 512-character limit.
- The proposed `new` exceeds the 512-character limit by 28 characters.

The supplied actual host result shows that the actual final action, with its 512-character `new`, was accepted. The proposed replacement, with its 540-character `new`, would not satisfy the documented `new` limit.
