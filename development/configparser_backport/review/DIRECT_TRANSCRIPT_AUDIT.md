## Supported interpretation and next question

The saved library repair survives actual context pressure and a later independent
check. Qwen also detects and repairs its own damaging edit from visible source
and diff. Those are real successes. The complete requested contribution fails:
after C18, another 120,000 generated tokens and 136.06 request minutes produce
no further saved change. Tests and documentation remain untouched.

The evidence earns examination of patch capacity and read granularity: the
damaged 512-character final, repeated manual counting, evidence rotation and
latest-result admission stop are concrete observations. It does not earn a
whole-host rewrite, automatic summaries, a working account or a reasoning cap.
Long speculative planning already occurred before externalization; absent
private plans, externally stored source and incorrect use of visible source
remain distinct mechanisms. Successful retrieval alone would not resolve all
three. Use a small neutral Qwen consultation before settling an intervention,
and evaluate its consequences on saved, checked work rather than accepted
actions alone. Retaining an aspect of the interface remains a valid outcome.
