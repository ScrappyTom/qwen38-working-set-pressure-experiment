## Decision

Retain the tool reference, exact storage/recovery, guards and current
presentation. **No metadata refactor, protected-group mechanism, working account,
reasoning change or larger live working-set allowance is adopted.** The model's
preference is useful input, and our capacity findings do not obligate an interface
change merely because a compact view can be imagined.

The next preparation should test continuation from the saved, checked optimizer
contribution into the historical report. It must first qualify a concrete
evidence-and-target view under an explicitly selected allowance. Keep a budget-only
option and a compact working view distinct; choose the smallest intervention
that can actually supply the needed material through feedback. This bounded
development task would be assisted and reused, with no new claim of independent
discovery or unchanged Experiment 020 replication. The details and remaining
execution boundary are in [NEXT_STEPS.md](NEXT_STEPS.md).

The dialogue cost **48.05 request minutes and 46,915 generated tokens**. Both
responses mix useful analysis with repeated reconsideration. That cost is
preserved; it does not establish its causal source or justify another discussion
merely to shorten an answer. The two-turn scope is consumed. There is no D3,
automatic successor or rescue of the closed pressure comparison.

The inherited receipt flags used the wrong reserve constant. Their original
values remain sealed, while verification derives the correct selected-reserve
comparison. Both outputs fit 32,768 and finish without truncation. Sampled GPU
free memory reached 191 MiB under the accepted advisory policy; D1 also overlapped
offline tokenizer work, so no isolated inference-speed or future memory-stability
claim follows. See the [execution receipt](EXECUTION_RECEIPT.md),
