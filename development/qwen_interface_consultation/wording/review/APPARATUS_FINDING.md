# Apparatus finding — valid execution, limited behavioral sensitivity

The completed attempt is mechanically interpretable as the prepared four-pair
development comparison. All eight native inputs matched preparation before
dispatch; each actual response, action, result and state is sealed. The independent
verifier confirms the complete chain, unchanged sources and earlier closures,
paired-input isolation, native/endpoint/offline accounting, and eight exact fresh
state replays. No runtime, protocol, transport, guard or custody failure invalidates
the observed comparison. The five reviews include full direct transcript reading,
not only this mechanical conclusion.

This does not establish a general improvement in operational understanding or
performance. Both conditions now have the complete visible tool reference. The
new controls do not reproduce the earlier check-first obligation or executed
navigation mistake, although two I3 controls briefly question navigation/reading
requirements before acting correctly. Sensitivity to the historical target is
therefore limited. Absence of the error in both conditions proves neither efficacy
nor inefficacy of the candidate. The two edits are one package; these results do
not isolate either change.

The same previously exposed I3/I4 development states were reused with new seeds,
not fresh capability tasks. Original model answers, prior audits, evaluator truth
and preferred next actions were not inserted into the actual inputs. Four other
prepared native regressions remained offline; their token/byte checks are not
behavioral safety evidence for crowded or old-check situations.

No actor crosses a context boundary here. Maximum live input is 3,906 tokens,
maximum output 1,107, minimum physical space left 51,851. The q4/56,576 run's
339 MiB sampled minimum is an observed monitored margin, not passage of the
original 350 MiB threshold or qualification of future difficult 25k work.
All comparisons used the same actor, KV, sampler, reasoning and context settings;
there was no MTP, cap, rescue, cache reuse or preset switch.

Tool outcomes are eight accepted actions, including four task-conforming literal
edits and four useful acquisitions. There are zero public checks and zero
submissions. No result was subsequently shown to the actor, so retrieval does
not establish use of the old marker, source reading does not establish eventual
historical recovery, and a planned repair/check/submission is not task completion.
The host withheld all continuations by design. Correction opportunity was not
exercised; the displayed fixture allowance must not be mistaken for eight live
multi-turn sessions.

Three candidate responses were shorter and faster, but the fourth more than
offset those savings. Aggregate generation/time rose, while the two I4 pairs
chose different useful acquisition orders. Small, heterogeneous one-action pairs
cannot establish whole-task efficiency, a dependable latency effect or a preferred
acquisition order. Exact response length, request time and token input costs are
reported separately, including the mixed pair, without post-hoc trimming.

The presentation decision may carry the source-checked wording corrections into
the next prepared interface on the prior direct ambiguity evidence and accurate
host semantics. This is a documented design judgment, not a promotion justified
by measured speedup or proof that confusion has been eliminated. Complete tool
information remains required. The shared host and historical frozen interfaces
are unchanged. No further micro-test, new representation or live successor is
automatically authorized.
