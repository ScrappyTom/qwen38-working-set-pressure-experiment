and 23,808-input resident ceiling are arithmetically compatible with C=56,576 and
G=32,768. This candidate is not eligible for the primary pressure comparison.

## What the candidate establishes offline

The seven-file, 4,101-byte application implements two legitimate decimal-rounding
policies. Two authored incident worlds share exact initial source/configuration
and task. Actual application executions produce the captured records. The
external receiving agreements differ. Each world therefore requires a different
targeted job assignment. The correct change passes all 31 public cases in its
world; the original, wrong-job change and blanket change fail. The eight-cell
cross-check matrix is in `preparation-001/counterfactual-checks.json`.

This is a concrete improvement in information requirements: noticing a suspicious
source statement cannot determine the justified repair. The actor could retrieve
the saved capture/agreement or use the public check's current execution and
failures. The latter is an intended diagnostic route, not evidence leakage to
conceal. A source-only guess can still be lucky. These checks establish task
structure, not model investigation, hypothesis revision or causal evidence use.
